How to read these prices

  • Prices are per linear metre of board, ex VAT, the same for everyone whether you order one board or a pallet.
  • Boards are supplied in random lengths from stock unless you tell us the length you would ideally like.
  • Setup fees apply under 20 m²: £60 for machining each product, and £150 for each coating colour. Every product in the same coating counts together towards its 20 m², so one colour across several profiles pays the coating fee once at most.
  • 42 x 42 boards, and 142 x 20 Scottish larch, are cut in pairs from a larger block, so they are sold in multiples of two pieces of each length.
  • Battens, screws, ventilation strip and delivery are priced with your quote. Collection from the mill at Pomathorn Farm, Penicuik EH26 8PJ is free, any time 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday.
  • Standard lead time is two weeks from payment.

To get a quote, send us the species, profile, size, finish and the area or the elevation drawings, with the delivery address if you would like it delivered.
